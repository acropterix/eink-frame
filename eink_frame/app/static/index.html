Pictures

"Show next" puts a picture on the frame at its next check-in. A date (MM-DD) shows it all day every year, like a birthday.

Frame settings

Change the frame's network settings without opening it. Fill in only what you want to change. The frame picks them up at its next check-in and tries them on the following wake. If it can't connect, it goes back to its previous settings and reports the problem here.

Frame firmware

Upload eink_frame.ino.bin from Arduino's Sketch → Export Compiled Binary. The frame installs it at its next check-in if its battery is above 3.7 V (or it has no battery reading), then restarts.
